Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Life Journal AI is a personal journaling application designed to help you record thoughts, moods, daily experiences, and reflections. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information the application may handle and how that information is used.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, Life Journal AI may handle information you enter into the app, including journal entries, mood selections, challenges, activities, gratitude, ratings, and other reflection information.

2. Account Information

If you use account or sign-in features, basic account information associated with your sign-in may be processed so that your account and journal data can be associated with you.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your information may be used to provide journaling features, display your history, generate statistics and progress information, provide search and calendar functionality, and provide AI-powered reflection features that you choose to use.

4. AI Features

When you use an AI feature, relevant information may be processed by the AI service used by the application to generate the requested response. Avoid entering information into an AI feature that you do not want processed for that purpose.

5. Cloud and Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on third-party services for authentication, cloud storage, analytics, or AI processing. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain available while your account or stored journal data is maintained. If you want your account or stored data deleted, contact the application owner through the contact method provided with the app or website.

8. Children's Privacy

Life Journal AI is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly request personal information from children in violation of applicable requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, contact the Life Journal AI owner through the official contact channel provided with the application or website.
